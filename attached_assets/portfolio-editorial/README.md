## Generation prompts

### Education workflow

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a premium landscape editorial image for a Malawian programmer's professional portfolio, illustrating practical education technology delivery. A restrained real-world education consultancy workspace, two fictional adult Black Southern African colleagues reviewing a laptop at a simple well-lit desk, side or over-the-shoulder framing with the screen out of focus. Soft natural window light, understated navy and warm neutral tones, clean, intentional composition, professional candid mood. Wide landscape 3:2, central framing suitable for a 16:9 crop. No legible text, brands, recognizable real people, official insignia, watermarks, claims, or staged handshakes. This is a conceptual illustration, not a named client or documentary photo.

### Internal operations

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a premium landscape editorial image for a Malawian software portfolio illustrating internal operations and accountable software delivery. Close three-quarter still life of a modern laptop showing abstract, softly defocused interface elements, a small closed notebook, and daylight falling across a charcoal desk. Restrained navy, graphite and warm neutral tones with a subtle cool-blue accent, believable materials, polished magazine photography, a clearly defined composition and enough contrast for a dark website. Wide landscape 3:2, central framing suitable for a 16:9 crop. No legible text, brands, people, flags, watermarks, specific metrics, or recognizable interface. This is a conceptual illustration, not a product screenshot.
