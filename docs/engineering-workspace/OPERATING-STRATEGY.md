# Operating strategy: skill, delivery and commercial accountability

The objective is to turn repeatable engineering ability into reliable delivery, useful public proof and a business that depends less on the founder. Adding panels cannot establish that outcome. Use observed delivery, collected cash, direct costs, retained customers and delegated completion as evidence. Keep personal practice, company economics and nonprofit stewardship distinct.

## Daily operating loop

1. Write the day's engineering objective and the constraint that matters: customer delivery, reliability, recurring margin, learning or delegation.
2. Select no more than three committed results. Each task needs a named accountable person, dependencies, acceptance criteria and a due date when a deadline actually exists. The current owner-only coordination model records accountability; organizational assignment and notifications are a later integration.
3. Record actual work in the time ledger, linked to its task or investigation. Record a result or obstacle rather than treating hours as output. Do not count the diary reflection estimate again.
4. Log risks when new evidence changes likelihood, impact or the recovery path. Link an observation to an authorized asset before interpreting unfamiliar traffic as hostile.
5. Prepare meetings around decisions and alternatives. Capture minutes and create linked follow-up tasks. Rehearse presentations around the audience's intended decision rather than visual effects alone.
6. Close with the result, evidence, lesson, unresolved question and next action. Keep client information private. Prepare a separate public copy only when disclosure and claims have been reviewed.

## Weekly founder and delivery review

| Question | Evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What reached its acceptance criteria? | Completed task, result and evidence link | Ship, continue or repair |
| Where did founder time go? | Actual time entries by context/category, delivery result | Eliminate, document, automate or delegate a repeatable step |
| Which risk could stop delivery or recovery? | Risk score, trigger, owner, mitigation and review date | Fund the smallest useful control; exercise recovery |
| Which communication needs action? | Existing communications workspace, meeting minutes and linked task | Reply through the authorized channel; no automatic sending from the new workspace |
| What did an AI tool cost and get wrong? | Reported provider/model/cost, human verdict and verification notes | Retain, constrain or stop the use case; leave unknown cost unknown |
| What should become public proof? | Reviewed walkthrough, transcript, permission to disclose, qualified evidence | Publish a sanitized snapshot; preserve limitations |
| What should be repeated next week? | Failed hypothesis, corrected exercise and observed result | Deliberate practice in an owned or explicitly authorized lab |

The dashboard's top three tasks use a simple disclosed rule: priority score plus overdue and blocked signals. It does not optimize company cash allocation or replace judgment about customer harm, obligations or dependencies.

## Monthly commercial review

Keep the established Aöthothe revenue strategy editable in its commercial workspace. Review service readiness, qualified demand, contracts, invoicing, collections, direct service cost, gross profit, recurring gross profit, retention and founder time. A pipeline value or invoice is not collected cash. Do not copy financial projections into this ledger as verified outcomes.

Evaluate a technology choice with a baseline and a comparison period:

`verified monthly net benefit = realized avoided expense + attributable collected contribution − incremental hosting/provider/support cost`

Time released is capacity until there is evidence of an expense reduction or a productive use. Report MWK amounts, period, source, uncertainty and the person who reviewed the calculation. Reliability benefits may require recovery time, lost-work incidents or support rework measures rather than a claimed monetary saving.

For SUDERN, keep programme objectives, beneficiary information, permissions, safeguarding and restricted funds in the institutional system. A working portal does not prove programme delivery or impact. Public portfolio statements must identify which evidence is software evidence and which is independently established institutional evidence.

## Behavioral discipline

Use the diary and practice records for self-authored principles, commitments and reflection: the hypothesis, the emotional or procedural obstacle you chose to record, the response and the next observable behavior. This is a private review habit, not psychological profiling or automated personality control. A polished interface and a wealth label do not substitute for cash discipline, ethical judgment, technical competence or client delivery.

## Staged expansion and acceptance

| Sequence | Deliverable | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Release this foundation | Journal plus private records, ownership and public-review boundary | Passing source checks; isolated migration; dependency audit resolved or an accountable separately authorized exception; authenticated exact-source deployment checks |
| Operate real work for two weeks | Actual diary/tasks/time/risks/decisions and one reviewed walkthrough per chosen discipline | No invented posts, hours, savings or completion; weekly review can reconstruct decisions from evidence |
| Establish integration contracts | Choose source of truth for contacts/tasks/calendar, AI and financial reporting | Named owner, permitted fields, least privilege, failure/retry behavior, cost limit, audit and revocation path per connector |
| Pilot observability on owned assets | One endpoint/server and one cloud application, with approved collection | Authorization, asset-to-source mapping, signed/authenticated ingestion, replay defense, missing-feed alerts, redaction, retention and alert-delivery test |
| Exercise response | Baseline traffic, triage an unexpected event and test backup recovery | Reviewed indicators, false-positive assessment, recovery proof and a human-approved rollback; no automatic blanket blocking |
| Expand only on measured value | Managed service offering and delegated runbooks | Paying customer evidence, support capacity, service objectives, direct cost and gross margin, repeatable handoff and restore exercises |

Cybersecurity and offensive practice belong in isolated labs or within written authorization defining systems, techniques, timing and stop conditions. Study network diagnosis, secure coding, identity, backups, logs and incident response alongside exploit mechanics. Embedded/mobile work also needs device ownership, safe handling and reproducible firmware/interface evidence. No scanning or attack tooling is activated by this change.

Each further automation should remove a measured bottleneck. Avoid integrating every platform before the operating loop produces reliable records and a proven commercial need.
